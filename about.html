☕ About Hotel Malnad Coffee House Hotel Malnad Coffee House is a friendly and welcoming food destination located in the beautiful city of Chikkamagaluru, Karnataka. The hotel was started by Preetham H B with a simple dream of serving tasty food at affordable prices. Our hotel brings together traditional South Indian flavours and a comfortable atmosphere. We believe that good food does not always have to be expensive. Every customer who walks through our doors is treated with care and respect. Our menu includes a variety of popular South Indian dishes. Customers can enjoy hot idlis, crispy vadas, delicious dosas and tasty meals. Our special filter coffee is one of the highlights of the hotel. Freshly prepared coffee gives customers the perfect way to start their morning. The aroma of hot coffee welcomes everyone who visits our hotel. We use fresh ingredients to prepare our dishes every day. Our cooks take special care to maintain the taste and quality of every meal. We want every plate served at our hotel to bring a smile to our customers. The hotel is designed to provide a simple and comfortable dining experience. It is a place where students can enjoy breakfast before college. It is also a place where families can spend time together over a good meal. Travellers visiting Chikkamagaluru can stop by for a quick and tasty meal. Local people can enjoy their favourite food in a friendly environment. Our staff always tries to provide quick and polite service. We believe that customer satisfaction is one of the most important parts of our business. Our hotel is inspired by the food culture and natural beauty of Chikkamagaluru. The city is famous for its beautiful hills, greenery and coffee plantations. We wanted our hotel to carry a small part of that local feeling. Our coffee is therefore an important part of our identity. Along with coffee, we offer a variety of snacks and traditional dishes. Our breakfast menu is perfect for people looking for a fresh and filling start to their day. Our lunch menu offers simple and delicious South Indian meals. In the evening, customers can enjoy snacks along with a hot cup of tea or coffee. We try to keep our prices affordable for everyone. Quality, cleanliness and good service are our main priorities. Our goal is not only to serve food but also to create a memorable experience. We want customers to feel comfortable whenever they visit us. Many people enjoy meeting their friends over a cup of coffee. Some customers visit us every morning for their regular breakfast. Others visit with their families during weekends. Every customer becomes a small part of the story of our hotel. We are thankful to everyone who supports our journey. The hotel continues to grow with the support of local customers.
